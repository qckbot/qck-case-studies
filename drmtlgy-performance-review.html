And the data shows exactly that compounding effect: collection pages built out through this engagement convert organic visitors at roughly 10% to purchase, while blog pages convert at roughly 5%.

Organic Performance Summary

Total organic-search-attributed revenue fell from $8.05M to $5.12M year-over-year, a decline of roughly 36.5%, tracking closely with a 38.8% drop in organic sessions. Digging into Search Console and GA4 side by side, this is not a conversion problem — revenue-per-session and conversion rate held flat or improved on every major page. It’s a visibility problem, and it has two distinct, identifiable causes.

Average position and conversion rate have both been improving, while clicks and sales haven’t followed at the same pace yet. Position, CTR, clicks, and sales move in sequence — each stage lags the one before it.

Breaking that down further: the click decline is concentrated entirely in branded search. Non-branded clicks — driven by category and informational content — are actually trending up over the same 3-month window, a direct reflection of the content program working as intended.

Long-Tail Keyword Footprint

Position tracking shows a clear, sustained pattern: rankings in the top 3 have held steady or improved, while rankings in positions 4–10 have eroded gradually over the past year — down roughly 74% in that band. Fewer, narrower rankings mean a much better average position on paper, but a smaller pool of total search visibility to draw clicks from.

Product Rich-Result Visibility

Search Console’s Search Appearance data shows a near-identical pattern on both the homepage and the highest-value product pages: “Product snippet” impressions — the rich results showing image, price, and rating directly in search — collapsed by 93–95%.

This single factor accounts for the large majority of the impression loss on the SPF-fair page specifically, and losing this kind of rich result costs the highest-intent clicks first — a shopper who already saw price and rating and clicked anyway converts at a very different rate than someone clicking a plain blue link.

A related, separate signal: branded search impressions (“drmtlgy” and variants) are actually up 60% year-over-year — more people are searching the brand name than ever — but click-through on those branded terms has fallen even while holding the #1 position. That points to something intercepting clicks on the SERP itself (ad competition, reputation-site placement, or SERP layout changes) rather than any loss of brand-term ranking strength.

Position has moved from the 30s–40s into the ~5–15 range across major pages — a real, substantial improvement. That improvement hasn’t fully worked its way through to clicks yet, for a few compounding reasons:

Seasonality

Skincare search demand softens in late spring/summer, so even strong rankings are pulling from a smaller overall search pool during part of this window.

AI Overviews & SERP features

A broader, industry-wide shift — AI-generated answers and richer SERP layouts are increasingly satisfying queries before a click ever happens, independent of ranking quality.

Keyword mix

Some of the strongest ranking gains sit on lower-volume terms, so improved position doesn’t mechanically translate into a proportional volume of new clicks.

Rich-result loss

The Product snippet collapse independently suppresses clicks even where ranking position itself is strong.

Next Priorities

  • Collection page scaling: the conversion data is clear — collection pages convert at ~10% of organic traffic. Continuing to build out and rank new collection pages is the highest-leverage growth lever.
  • Rich-result recovery: audit and repair product schema markup / Merchant Center feed health to restore lost Product snippet visibility on the homepage and top product pages.
  • Brand SERP defense: investigate what’s now occupying the SERP above the organic brand listing — paid conquesting, reputation content, or layout shifts — to recover branded click-through.
  • Category term expansion: formalize tracking and continued investment in the eye-corrector and neck-cream category terms already ranking top-4 organically without dedicated targeting to date.
  • New keyword territory: expand into high-volume, low-difficulty gaps (color corrector, dark spot corrector, skin barrier repair, hyaluronic acid serum) where the brand currently has zero visibility.
  • AI-channel investment: continue building the AEO/GEO foundation that’s already driving outsized early returns via ChatGPT referral traffic.
