How We Work
Ghostwriting, perfected through systems.

Writing for expert audiences is not a matter of effort - it is a matter of process. Here is exactly how every piece of QCK content is built, reviewed, and approved.

Step 01
✍️

The Writer

Every client is assigned a dedicated writer with relevant domain knowledge. They use a custom-built AI research assistant - tuned to your brand voice and messaging guide - to power the research phase. The actual writing is done by a human, every time. No AI-generated copy goes to clients.

Writer
Step 02
🔍

The Editor

A dedicated editor reviews every draft before it moves forward. Their job: verify dos and don'ts from your messaging guide are followed, confirm tone and voice are on-brand, and catch anything a subject-matter expert in your audience would catch. Nothing moves without their sign-off.

Editor
Step 03
✅

The Content Manager

The content manager is your primary point of contact and the final quality gate. They review for flow, consistency, and strategic alignment - and coordinate feedback loops in month one until every piece feels like it came from inside your team.

Content Manager
Additional Details

Batched delivery, not a firehose. Content goes live in scheduled batches throughout the month - not all at once. This keeps quality consistent and your editorial calendar manageable.

Messaging guide built before kickoff. Before a single word is written, we document your tone, voice, dos, don'ts, and brand-specific rules. This guide is the writer and editor's bible.

Client validation built into month one. We treat the first 30 days as a calibration period. Your feedback directly updates the messaging guide so every subsequent piece gets sharper.

Your team can meet the whole crew. Most clients work directly with their content manager. But if you want to pull in the writer and editor for a deeper review session, that's always on the table.
